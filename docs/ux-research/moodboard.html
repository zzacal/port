Gallery: UX reference board

These are twelve live portfolios by individual photographers, screenshotted on 2026-10-29 at 1440×900. They are grouped by how closely they match our structure (home → gallery → single image). For each one, the caption says what is worth borrowing. Raw research notes are in candidates-a.md and candidates-b.md.

Three directions to choose from

A. Contact sheet

Kenna + Angibaud + Kander solo view

  • Home: a grid of gallery covers, small, with title and count under each
  • Gallery: an even grid of small thumbnails at natural aspect ratios
  • Solo: one image on white, a caption line, text prev/next
  • Feels like a printed contact sheet: orderly and archival

B. Monograph

Kawauchi + del Marmol

  • Home: a text index of galleries on the left, with one large featured image
  • Gallery: one large image at a time with a thin list of thumbnails
  • Solo: the image fills the page, with a quiet side column of text
  • Feels like a photo book: slow and immersive

C. Film strip

Thies + Mitchell

  • Home: one row per gallery showing 4–5 small frames, not a single cover
  • Gallery: a dense contact grid
  • Solo: as in A
  • Shows more of the work up front; a busier home page
