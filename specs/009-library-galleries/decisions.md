Source: DEFAULT means the recommended option was taken because the owner was away; OWNER means the
owner chose it. Pivot cost is the cost to reverse after implementation.

| 1 | Seeded content removal: retire the seed command, manifest and seed-only code; add a one-time removal command, guarded to development targets, that deletes seeded images (as image deletes) and seeded galleries (as gallery deletes) and reports counts.
